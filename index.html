I'm Navjot Singh, a Graphic Designer based in Dhuri, Punjab, India. With over a year of hands-on experience, I specialize in creating compelling visual identities that help brands stand out in a crowded digital world.

From designing eye-catching posters and banners to crafting memorable logos and social media creatives, I bring creativity and precision to every project. My goal is to deliver designs that not only look stunning but also communicate your message effectively.

Proficient in industry-standard tools — CorelDRAW, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, and Canva — I ensure print-ready quality and pixel-perfect digital outputs.
